FossScout helps you discover free and open source Android software, check an app against verified FOSS store indexes, and hand off to a trusted store client without guesswork.

It is local-first by design: no account, no ads, no analytics, and no hidden telemetry. Your favourites, notes, collections, and settings stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Browse a curated catalog of FOSS projects and filter by category or tag.
• Search by project name, tagline, or tag.
• Check an app by name or package against verified F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid indexes.
• Review match confidence, evidence, coverage, and alternatives.
• Correct a wrong match locally and keep a private note.
• Inspect store security details, pinned fingerprints, index signatures, and verification history.
• Save favourites, write private notes, and build collections you can share or import.
• Export and restore your local profile with an optional passphrase.
• Hand off to F-Droid, Droid-ify, or Neo Store, or open a browser fallback.
• Report a missing store, wrong match, or adapter request with a redacted preview.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
FossScout keeps its working data on your device. The Play build uses manual package checks and limited package visibility; it does not request broad package access, install packages, or use an accessibility service. The F-Droid build adds a full installed-app scan for people who want it.

Network access is used for store index sync and for links you choose to open. Store indexes are checked against pinned fingerprints and signatures. Reports are never uploaded automatically: you review the redacted content and decide whether to copy, share, or open it in your browser.

FossScout does not download or install APKs. It helps you find the source and hand off to a store client you trust.

FossScout is free software under the MIT License. The catalog is made of openly licensed projects.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
