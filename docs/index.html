FossScout

Discover free and open source software

FossScout is an Android app that combines a curated FOSS catalog, installed-app inventory, verified store indexes, and local comparisons in a Material 3 interface.

No account. No ads. No analytics. Your favourites, notes, collections, and settings stay on your device.

View source on GitHub Privacy policy

Local-first

FossScout uses public store indexes and processes app information on your device. It does not upload your installed-app inventory, notes, favourites, or collections.

Project

FossScout is free software maintained by FossScout contributors. Read the project README for features, build profiles, and development instructions, or report an issue.
